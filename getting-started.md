# Getting Started

A step-by-step guide to setting up your AI-powered job search. No prior experience with AI tools required.

## What you'll need

You'll need:

- an account with an AI assistant, such as Claude or ChatGPT
- your CV
- for Claude's daily searches, a paid Claude plan (the Chrome extension needs Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise; check Claude's help pages for which plans include Cowork)

## A note on privacy

Before you share personal information, here's how your data is handled:

- whichever AI service you use, such as Claude or ChatGPT, processes your data, so read its privacy policy to see how it handles and keeps it
- for Claude, read [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)
- you can leave out sensitive information, such as salary details or personality results, and the toolkit still works, just with less personalisation
- nothing in this toolkit sends your data anywhere beyond the AI service you're already using

## Pick your route

Pick the one that matches the AI tool you use:

- **Any AI assistant, such as ChatGPT or Gemini.** One page covers setup and your daily searches:
  - [Set up with any AI assistant](setup-any-ai-assistant.md)
- **Claude in the browser or desktop app.** Two parts:
  - [Part 1: set up your project](setup-claude-app.md)
  - [Part 2: your daily searches](daily-searches.md)
- **Claude Code.** Two parts:
  - [Part 1: set up your project](setup-claude-code.md)
  - [Part 2: your daily searches](daily-searches.md)

Not sure which? If you use Claude and have never opened a terminal (a window where you type commands), pick the Claude app. You don't need to be a developer for any of them.

## Your own copy on GitHub (optional)

You don't need a GitHub account to use the toolkit. If you have one:

- **Make your own copy.** On the [toolkit's GitHub page](https://github.com/kerriel/ai-job-search-toolkit), click **Use this template**, then **Create a new repository**, and choose **Private**. Your copy starts private. Even so, keep your CV and profile in your separate `job-search` folder, not in this copy.
- **Fork it only if you want to suggest improvements.** A fork is a public copy linked to the original: click **Fork** at the top of the [toolkit's GitHub page](https://github.com/kerriel/ai-job-search-toolkit), then see [how to contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md).

> [!CAUTION]
> A fork of a public repository stays public, and GitHub won't let you make it private. Never save any personal information to a fork: no CV, no name, no salary expectations, nothing about you.
