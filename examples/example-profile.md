# Example Profile

This is an example of a finished profile, so you can see what you'll end up with. You don't fill it in yourself: your AI assistant writes yours with you, one question at a time, when you run the [profile builder prompt](../prompts/01-profile-builder.md).

**Keep it fresh.** Your profile will change over time. If your preferences, dealbreakers, salary expectations or circumstances change, tell your AI assistant and say: `Update my profile and give it to me in a grey box.` In Claude Code, it updates the file for you. In the browser or desktop app, save it over `my-profile.md`, remove the old copy from your project knowledge and upload the new one. An out-of-date profile means out-of-date recommendations.

# My Profile

## About me
**Name:** Sam Chen
**Location:** Manchester, UK
**Career stage:** Mid-career (8 years of experience)
**Professional summary:** Operations and programme manager with experience across logistics, supply chain, and software-as-a-service (SaaS) platforms. Started in warehouse operations, moved into programme management, and now leads operational improvement initiatives at a mid-stage logistics tech company. Comfortable with both hands-on process work and strategic planning.

## Experience and skills

### Domain knowledge
- Logistics and supply chain operations
- Warehouse management systems
- Last-mile delivery optimisation
- SaaS platforms for fleet management
- E-commerce fulfilment workflows

### Key skills
- Programme management across multiple workstreams
- Process mapping and operational improvement
- Stakeholder management across engineering, commercial, and operations teams
- Data analysis and reporting (Excel, Looker, and basic SQL for pulling data from databases)
- Vendor and partner management
- Change management and running training
- Budget tracking and resource allocation

### Track record
- Reduced order processing time by 35% across three fulfilment centres
- Led migration from an old warehouse management system (WMS) to a cloud-based one serving 200+ users
- Cut operational costs by £180k annually through route optimisation project
- Built and trained a team of 6 programme coordinators from scratch

## What I'm looking for

### Target roles
- Operations Manager
- Programme Manager
- Head of Operations
- Supply Chain Manager

### Target industries
**Interested in:** Logistics tech, supply chain, climate tech, e-commerce
**Avoiding:** Gambling, cryptocurrency, fast fashion

### Working arrangement
Hybrid preferred (2–3 days in office maximum). Open to fully remote. Office must be within commuting distance of Manchester. Would consider a 4-day week if offered but not a dealbreaker.

### What matters most
1. Growth opportunity into a Head of Operations or Director role within 2 years
2. Working with a strong senior team who value operations
3. Interesting, complex operational problems
4. Good work-life balance
5. Mission-driven company (doing some good preferred but not essential)

## Hard dealbreakers
- Salary below £55,000
- Office-based roles with no flexibility (must offer at least 2 days remote)
- Roles requiring hands-on software development
- Companies in gambling, cryptocurrency, or fast fashion
- Commute longer than 1 hour each way

## Strong preferences
- Company stage: scale-up or established (not very early stage)
- Direct reports or team leadership responsibilities
- Clear progression path documented
- Climate tech or companies doing social good

## Personality and working style
**16 Personalities:** ISTJ-A (Assertive Logistician)
**Enneagram:** Type 1 (The Reformer) with a 2 wing
**CliftonStrengths top 5:** Achiever, Responsibility, Analytical, Deliberative, Consistency

*Coaching notes: Sam is methodical and process-driven. Responds well to structured feedback with clear data points. When facing a setback, such as a rejection, tends to go quiet and overthink rather than talking it through. Providing concrete next steps and evidence-based reassurance works better than emotional encouragement. Tends towards conservative decisions. Occasionally worth challenging: "What would you do if this couldn't fail?"*

## Communication preferences
- Writing style: direct, no fluff
- Tone for cover letters: confident but not boastful
- Language: UK English
- Banned words: passionate, utilise, leverage, synergy, rockstar, ninja
- Formatting: bullet points over long paragraphs where possible
- No em dashes
