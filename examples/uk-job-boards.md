# UK Job Boards and Review Sites

Use this page to choose which UK job boards to set up searches for. The job search prompt has built-in steps for LinkedIn, Indeed UK and Totaljobs. For any other board, choose **Other** when it asks which board you're searching, and describe how the board works.

## Job boards

### LinkedIn

- **Website:** linkedin.com/jobs
- **Best for:** Professional and senior roles, networking-driven searches, direct employer postings
- **Notes:** It has strong filters, and saved searches tell you about new matches. The "Easy Apply" option is widely used, but some employers prefer applications through their own site. It carries a mix of direct employer and recruiter postings.

### Indeed UK

- **Website:** uk.indeed.com
- **Best for:** High volume of listings across all levels, good date filtering, salary transparency
- **Notes:** It's good for casting a wide net, and you can sort by date to see the newest listings first. Salary is shown on many listings. It carries a mix of direct employer and recruiter postings, with more from recruiters than LinkedIn.

### Totaljobs

- **Website:** totaljobs.com
- **Best for:** Mid-level to senior roles, strong in technology, finance, and operations
- **Notes:** It's a UK-focused board with good filters, including employment type (permanent or contract). Listings show a card preview before the full description, which is useful for quick screening. Many listings are from recruitment agencies.

### Reed

- **Website:** reed.co.uk
- **Best for:** Broad coverage across industries and levels, particularly strong for public sector and operations roles
- **Notes:** You can register your CV on Reed, and recruiters can search it. It carries a mix of listings, with a strong recruiter presence.

### Guardian Jobs

- **Website:** jobs.theguardian.com
- **Best for:** Charity, non-profit, public sector, media, and education roles
- **Notes:** It has fewer listings, mostly posted by employers directly, but it's strong for mission-driven organisations. If you're looking for social impact roles, it's worth checking regularly.

## Company review site: Glassdoor

### Glassdoor UK

- **Website:** glassdoor.co.uk
- **Best for:** Company ratings, salary data, interview experience reports
- **What to look for:**
  - Overall rating (out of 5)
  - "Would recommend to a friend" percentage
  - Work/life balance rating
  - Culture and values score
  - Senior management score
  - Recent employee reviews (sorted by most recent)
- **Limitations:**
  - Review numbers vary hugely, so a company with 500 reviews gives a very different signal from one with 5
  - Check how recent the reviews are: a company with poor reviews a few years ago and strong ones this year may have improved, or changed its leaders
  - Reviews come mostly from people with strong opinions, good or bad, so people in the middle are under-represented
  - Some companies actively manage their Glassdoor presence, encouraging happy employees to post
  - Small companies may have very few reviews, making any single review disproportionately influential

## Recruiter vs direct employer postings

Recruiter postings are a normal part of the UK job market. Good recruiters know the hiring manager, can share context not in the listing, and can advocate for you. The search prompts in this repo flag whether a listing is from a recruiter or direct employer so you know what you're looking at.

## Which boards suit your level and industry

- **Graduate and entry-level:** Indeed UK, Reed, and LinkedIn tend to have the most volume
- **Mid-career:** All five boards are relevant. Totaljobs and Reed are strong for operations, finance, and technology
- **Senior and executive:** LinkedIn and direct approaches tend to work better than job boards. Guardian Jobs is worth checking for senior charity and public sector roles
- **Tech:** LinkedIn and Indeed UK. Also consider specialist boards such as Welcome to the Jungle (uk.welcometothejungle.com, formerly Otta)
- **Non-profit and public sector:** Guardian Jobs and CharityJob (charityjob.co.uk)
- **Startups:** LinkedIn, Welcome to the Jungle (uk.welcometothejungle.com, formerly Otta) and Wellfound (wellfound.com, formerly AngelList Talent)
